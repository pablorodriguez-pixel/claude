Founderz feature / benefit card — icon, purple title and a muted description; pin an image to the base with `image`.

```jsx
<FeatureCard icon={<Icon name="sparkles"/>} title="IA aplicada">
  Aprende a integrar la IA en tu día a día profesional.
</FeatureCard>
```

`surface`: white · grey · lilac. `bordered` adds the lilac hairline (card-f) for grids on dark sections.
